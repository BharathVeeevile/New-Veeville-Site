Studios & Culture

We are based in 2 cities renowned for design excellence – London and New York. Both studios are unique but our unrivaled culture ties us together. So whether you're enjoying rooftop gigs in New York, exhibitions at our studio in London or one of our trips all together, we are certain you'll fall in love with life at Pearlfisher.

Careers

At Veeville, we celebrate successes together by promoting a culture of gratitude, encouraging teamwork, and collaboration. We offer opportunities for professional development, support a healthy work-life balance, and foster diversity. If you are someone who appreciates a vibrant environment, then join us!

Client Servicing

You will be responsible for identifying new business opportunities, maintaining a relationship with clients, and having the ability to work with team members across departments.

Video Editor

You should have a passion for storytelling, a creative eye, and excellent technical skills. You are responsible for assembling raw footage into a finished product that meets the creative and technical requirements of the client.

Graphic Designer

You are responsible for creating and developing visual content that communicates the client's brand and message. You should have a strong understanding of design principles and be able to use a variety of software applications to create high-quality work.

Copywriter

You are responsible for writing clear, concise, and persuasive copy for a variety of marketing materials. You must have a strong understanding of the target audience and be able to write copy that resonates with them.
